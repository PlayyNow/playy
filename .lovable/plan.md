

# Playy — Social Sports App Landing Page Website

## Brand Voice & Positioning

**Voice:** Bold, energetic, inclusive, and action-oriented. Speaks like a teammate, not a corporation. Short punchy sentences. Gen-Z/millennial-friendly without trying too hard. Confident but never exclusive.

**Tagline Options:**
1. "Stop Scrolling. Start Playing."
2. "Your Next Game Is One Tap Away."
3. "Find Your People. Play Your Sport."

---

## Website Structure

**Primary Pages (single-page landing with scroll sections):**
- Home (Hero → How It Works → Sports → Benefits → Testimonials → FAQ → Download CTA)

**Optional future pages:**
- About / Our Story
- Blog (SEO content hub)
- Careers

We'll build this as a **single-page, high-converting landing page** — optimized for app downloads.

## Homepage — Section by Section

### 1. Hero Section
- **Headline:** "Stop Scrolling. Start Playing."
- **Subheading:** "Playy matches you with real players by sport, skill level, and availability. No group chat chaos. No mismatched games. Just play."
- **CTA Buttons:** "Download on App Store" + "Get it on Google Play"
- **Visual:** Full-bleed video/image background of diverse young athletes mid-action (padel, basketball, football). Gradient overlay with bold white text.
- **Design:** Dark background (#0A0A0A), neon green accent (#BFFF00), clean sans-serif typography

### 2. Social Proof Bar
- Animated counters: "10K+ Players" · "5,000+ Games Matched" · "4.8★ Rating"
- App Store / Google Play badges
- "As seen on" logos if available

### 3. How It Works (3-Step Process)
- **Step 1:** "Pick Your Sport" — Choose from padel, football, basketball, tennis, gym & more
- **Step 2:** "Match Your Level" — Get paired with players who actually match your skill
- **Step 3:** "Show Up & Play" — Confirmed spots, real times, real people
- Phone mockup animation showing the flow

### 4. Sports Grid
- Visual cards for each sport: Padel, Football, Basketball, Tennis, Gym
- Each card has an icon, sport name, and "Find Players →" micro-CTA
- Hover effect with sport-specific action photo

### 5. Benefits / Why Playy
- **"No More Group Chat Chaos"** — Organized games, confirmed players, zero back-and-forth
- **"Skill-Matched Games"** — Play with people at your level, every time
- **"Judge-Free Zone"** — A community built on inclusion, not clout
- **"Built for Action"** — Less scrolling, more playing
- Layout: alternating image + text rows with subtle scroll animations

### 6. Testimonials
- 3 rotating quote cards from early users
- Photo, name, sport, star rating
- "I found my regular padel crew in one week." — style quotes

### 7. App Preview / Screenshots
- Phone mockup carousel showing key screens
- Feature callouts: "Smart Matching," "Instant Booking," "Player Profiles"

### 8. FAQ Accordion
- "Is Playy free?" → Yes, free to download and use
- "What sports are available?" → Padel, football, basketball, tennis, gym & more coming
- "How does skill matching work?" → You set your level, we match you with similar players
- "Is Playy only in Dubai?" → We're launching in Dubai first, expanding soon
- "Is it safe?" → Verified profiles and community guidelines keep it judge-free

### 9. Final CTA Section
- **Headline:** "Your Next Game Is One Tap Away"
- **Subheading:** "Join thousands of players in Dubai. Download Playy now."
- App Store + Google Play buttons
- Instagram & TikTok social links

### 10. Footer
- Logo, tagline, social links (Instagram, TikTok)
- Links: Privacy Policy, Terms, Contact
- "Made with ❤️ in Dubai"

## Design Direction

- **Color Palette:** Jet black (#0A0A0A) background, electric lime green (#BFFF00) accent, white text, subtle gray (#1A1A1A) cards
- **Typography:** Bold geometric sans-serif (Inter/Space Grotesk style)
- **Feel:** Dark-mode-first, sporty, premium, app-like
- **Animations:** Fade-in on scroll, counter animations, subtle parallax on hero
- **Mobile-first:** Designed for the audience that lives on their phones
